You are the senior product architect for this project. Do not implement product features yet.

Read:
- README.md
- docs/00_PROJECT_CHARTER.md
- docs/01_PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md

Tasks:
1. Summarize the product in your own words.
2. List all assumptions that still need founder decisions.
3. Identify contradictions, missing requirements and risks.
4. Propose a minimal repository structure for the approved stack.
5. Propose a phase-by-phase implementation plan with dependencies.
6. Create `docs/11_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` and `docs/12_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`.
7. Do not scaffold code, install dependencies, create migrations or modify existing files beyond those two documents.
8. Ask the founder the smallest set of high-impact questions before proceeding.

Be precise. Do not invent market research, legal facts, payment-provider capabilities or business commitments.
